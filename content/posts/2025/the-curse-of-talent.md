# The Curse of Talent: Aaron Ofoyrwoth and the Rugby Cranes' Rollercoaster

## A Triumphant Debut in Luo Land (June 2019)

Serving a suspension as a school boy in June 2019, I decided to catch a bus to Luo land in neighbouring Kenya to catch the first leg of the decades' old rivalry (the Elgon Cup) at the Mamboleo Showground in Kisumu. It proved to be a successful hunting trip for the Rugby Cranes on enemy territory and also happened to be the first time I had seen the Cranes beat the Simbas live.

One player left me in awe and made fans wax lyrical - **Aaron Ofoyrwoth**. The young halfback proved to be the thorn in the Simbas' flesh, with a drop goal and penalty on the day enroute to a memorable **16-13 win**, earning himself **Man of the Match** recognition - a crowning ceremony for the Luo boy in Luo land.

## The Scapegoat Emerges (June 2025)

Fast forward to June 2025, following a **22-19 loss** for the Rugby Cranes against the Welwitschias (Namibia national rugby team) in Walvis Bay, a section of Ugandan fans quickly looked for a scapegoat. The once celebrated hero, Aaron Ofoyrwoth, turned out to be the juiciest target.

Despite his solid contribution off the bench, one incident kickstarted the online onslaught of the seasoned halfback - a **50/50 call** in the dying embers of the game meant he would sit out the last **10 minutes** on the naughty chair (sin bin).

## The Fan Reaction Divide
*   **Critics:**  
    > "Aaron prefers playing from the sin bin whenever he is wearing national colors." - *Fan on X*  
    > "We need to be honest regarding Aaron - He's not a good scrumhalf or flyhalf in 15s. He's very indisciplined......." - *Former teammate from the Mamboleo showdown*
*   **Defenders:**  
    Argued that Aaron played as a halfback in the Namibia encounter and was handed his debut and trusted early in his career by the seasoned **John Duncan** (architect behind Uganda's last Elgon Cup win in 2015) in the halfback positions, despite yellow card issues.

## The Talent and the Curse
> "One thing is for sure - talent comes with curses."

The talent of the seasoned halfback has gotten him to where he is but has also been a weapon conveniently used against him. Luckily, he is surrounded by men who have faced their fair share of curses and overcome them:

1.  **Philip Wokorach:**  
    Forced to sit out for close to a cumulative **2-year period** after 2 career-threatening injuries. He has since proven himself as **Uganda's greatest export**, evidenced by his performance in the inaugural **GMR Rugby Premier League** (a top-tier global 7s tournament).
    > ![Philip Wokorach receiving the Emerging player of the season award](/images/medal.jpg)  
    > *Philip Wokorach receiving the Emerging Player of the Season award at the inaugural GMR Rugby Premier League tournament*

2.  **Justin Kimono ('Pocket Rocket'):**  
    Suffered a horrendous knee injury in **2015** that threatened his career and ability to walk, forcing him out for **2 years straight**. Against all odds, he returned and earned selection for the recent test against Namibia.

## Redemption on the Horizon?
Going into the highly anticipated **Rugby Africa Cup**, the Ugandans will be hoping for a **repeat of the Mamboleo show** from the mercurial halfback, **Aaron Ofoyrwoth**, against the noisy neighbours (Kenya Simbas) on **8th July**.
